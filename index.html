    Rule ladder

    Atoms collide in water and wind. Correct CHON-rich mixes form molecules. Fe-S clusters catalyze polymers. Polymers plus phosphate membranes and thermal energy form protocells. Protocells ignite metabolic respiration to become motile swimming organisms that feed, grow, and reproduce by mitosis.
